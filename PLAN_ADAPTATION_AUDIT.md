## 1. Executive Summary

RunCoach has a genuinely strong skeleton: faithful Daniels VDOT core math, real CTL/ATL/TSB EWMA modeling, ACWR, high-water-mark deload tracking, structured rep/recovery workout steps that stay in lockstep with prose and distance, and a thoughtfully layered adaptation engine (hysteresis, overreach clamps, Bayesian shrinkage). In the parts that work, it is already near Runna/RunMotion quality. But several **correctness bugs silently invert the safety machinery**: the fatigue trend reads runs in DB order (can invert), the top race zone is paced *slower* than the zone below it in the VDOT-only path, I/R paces run too fast vs Daniels, CTL cold-starts biased-fresh for new runners, and load is intensity-blind for HR/RPE-less (Strava) runs. The biggest **methodology gaps** are structural: 8-12 week plans (the most common lengths) get **zero deload weeks**, the taper drops all intensity *and* gets inflated by positive adaptation, and low-run-count plans balloon "easy" runs to ~95-100% of the long run, breaking 80/20 for exactly the time-crunched runners who most need protection. The single biggest lever to compete with Runna/RunMotion is **getting the taper and deload cadence right** — periodization shape is the most visible quality signal and currently the most broken. Secondary high-leverage moves: make intensity prescription internally consistent (zone ladder, I/R paces, taper sharpening) and make load intensity-aware so adaptation can tell a polarized week from an all-hard one.

## 2. Competitive Position

| Capability | RunCoach today | Runna | RunMotion | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Structured workouts** | Real warm-up/reps/recovery/cool-down steps with VDOT paces; prose+steps+distance kept in lockstep | Coach-authored, watch-pushed | Per-session-distance pace lookup | Near-parity on *content*; lacks watch push & per-session-distance pacing |
| **Periodization** | Distance-aware phases, high-water deload tracking — but **no deload on ≤12wk plans**, flat blocks for fit/short, base detrains high-base runners | Two-phase + prepended base + taper | Reverse/elastic, auto-compress, final 12wk block | **Behind** — deload cadence & taper integrity are broken |
| **Taper** | Volume cut correct **but drops ALL intensity** and **gets scaled UP by positive adaptation** | Volume down, intensity kept | ~2wk lighter taper, intensity kept | **Behind** — taper not sacrosanct, no sharpening |
| **Auto-adaptation** | Rich: TSB clamps, overreach overrides, hysteresis, per-type ratios | Mileage Insights + Realignment | Trend-based + ACWR + heavy/down-week context | **At parity on engine**, but per-week (not intra-week) and rationale not surfaced |
| **Fatigue modeling** | Real CTL/ATL/TSB + ACWR, wired into clamps | Training-load comparison | Explicit ACWR + endurance index | **At parity on math**, undermined by cold-start bias + intensity-blind load |
| **Plan breadth** | Road/trail/ultra/beginner/fitness/performance; trail unusually deep | 5K→ultra, C25K, strength | +triathlon, multi-goal seasons | Good breadth; strength only on 1 of 4 generators |
| **Coaching voice** | Directive Coach's Note grounded in real signals; non-nagging | Per-session concrete rationale | Selectable tone | Note selector is excellent; per-session rationale is **static prose, no paces** |

**Honest strengths to keep (do not touch):** the Coach's Note focus selector, key-workout/distance lockstep, high-water deload *tracking*, TSB convention, and trail depth are all best-in-class.
