# 12-Week Training Plan for Quarter Ironman

## Quarter Ironman Distances
- **Swim:** 950m (0.6 miles)
- **Bike:** 45km (28 miles)
- **Run:** 10.5km (6.5 miles)
- **Total:** Approximately 35 miles

## Plan Overview

This 12-week plan is designed for athletes with a basic fitness foundation who want to complete a Quarter Ironman (often a step toward 70.3). The plan assumes you can currently:
- Swim 500m continuously
- Bike for 45 minutes
- Run for 30 minutes

### Training Volume
- **Week 1:** 4-5 hours
- **Peak Week:** 7-8 hours
- **Weekly structure:** 3 swims, 2-3 bikes, 2-3 runs, 1 brick

### Terminology

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **WU** | Warm-up |
| **MS** | Main Set |
| **CD** | Cool-down |
| **RI** | Rest Interval |
| **Low aerobic** | Very comfortable, conversational |
| **Moderate aerobic** | Comfortable but purposeful |
| **Threshold** | "Comfortably hard," sustainable for ~60 min |
| **VO2max** | Hard effort, sustainable for 3-8 minutes |

## Base Phase: Weeks 1-4

### Week 1

**Monday:** Rest

**Tuesday:**
- **Bike 45 min:** WU 10 min easy, MS 25 min moderate, CD 10 min easy

**Wednesday:**
- **Swim 1000m:** WU 200m, 4x25 drill, MS 500m moderate, CD 200m
- **Run 25 min:** Easy conversational pace

**Thursday:**
- **Bike 45 min:** Easy spin, low resistance

**Friday:**
- **Swim 1000m:** WU 200m, MS 4x150 (25 easy/25 fast), CD 200m

**Saturday:**
- **Run 30 min:** Easy with 4x20 sec pickups at end

**Sunday:**
- **Bike 60 min:** WU 10 min, MS 40 min moderate, CD 10 min
- **Brick Run 10 min:** Immediately off bike, easy pace

## Taper Phase: Week 12

### Week 12 (Race Week)

**Monday:** Rest

**Tuesday:**
- **Bike 45 min:** WU 10 min, MS 20 min easy with 2x2 min race pace, CD 10 min

**Wednesday:**
- **Swim 1000m:** WU 200m, MS pace work, a few short pickups, CD 200m
- **Run 25 min:** Easy with 4x30 sec pickups

**Thursday:**
- **Bike 30 min:** Easy spin, bike check

**Friday:**
- **Swim 800m:** Easy, visualization, practice sighting

**Saturday:** Rest or very light activity (swim 15 min if possible)

**Sunday: Race Day!**
- Swim: 950m
- Bike: 45km
- Run: 10.5km
