## Prerequisites by Distance

### Sprint Triathlon
- Comfortable swimming 500m continuously
- Able to ride a bike for 30 minutes
- Can run/walk 2 miles

### Olympic Triathlon
- Comfortable swimming 1000m continuously
- Regular cycling (weekly rides of 15+ miles)
- Running base of 10-15 miles/week

### Quarter Ironman
- Comfortable swimming 800m continuously
- Cycling base of 40-50 miles/week
- Running base of 15-20 miles/week

### Half Ironman (70.3)
- Comfortable swimming 1 hour
- Cycling 3x/week with 20+ mile long rides
- Running 3x/week with 5+ mile long runs

## Quick Start Recommendations

1. **Choose your distance** based on current fitness and available training time
2. **Assess your weakest sport** and prioritize it in early training
3. **Follow a structured plan** rather than random training
4. **Invest in proper gear** gradually, starting with essentials
5. **Practice nutrition** during training, not just on race day
6. **Join a community** - local tri club or training group

## Key Training Principles

### Periodization
Training is divided into distinct phases:
- **Base Phase:** Building aerobic endurance (long, slow training)
- **Build Phase:** Adding intensity and race-specific work
- **Peak Phase:** Maximum training load with race simulation
- **Taper Phase:** Reduced volume for race readiness

### Recovery
Recovery is when adaptation occurs. Key elements:
- Sleep 7-9 hours per night
- Take planned rest days
- Use recovery weeks every 3-4 weeks
- Monitor for overtraining signs

### Consistency
Regular, moderate training beats sporadic hard training. Aim for:
- 3 sessions per sport per week for longer distances
- 2 sessions per sport per week for shorter distances
- 1-2 strength sessions per week
