# 8-Week Training Plan for Sprint Triathlon

## Sprint Triathlon Distances
- **Swim:** 750m (0.47 miles)
- **Bike:** 20km (12.4 miles)
- **Run:** 5km (3.1 miles)
- **Total:** Approximately 16 miles

## Plan Overview

This 8-week plan is designed for beginners targeting their first sprint triathlon. It assumes you can currently:
- Swim 200m continuously
- Bike for 30 minutes
- Run/walk for 20 minutes

### Training Volume
- **Week 1:** 3-4 hours
- **Peak Week:** 6-7 hours
- **Weekly structure:** 2-3 swims, 2-3 bikes, 2-3 runs, 1 brick

### Terminology

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **WU** | Warm-up |
| **MS** | Main Set |
| **CD** | Cool-down |
| **RI** | Rest Interval |
| **Easy** | Conversational pace |
| **Moderate** | Comfortable challenge |
| **Hard** | Challenging but sustainable |

## Key Sessions to Prioritize

1. **Sunday brick** (bike + run)
2. **One longer swim** per week (Saturday or Sunday)
3. **One bike with intensity** per week
4. **One run with tempo** per week

## Beginner Tips

### Swimming
- If you can't swim 400m continuously yet, take extra swim lessons
- Focus on comfort in the water over speed
- Practice putting your face in the water
- Open water practice recommended before race

### Cycling
- Know your gears and how to use them
- Practice braking and cornering
- Learn to change a flat tire
- Test your race nutrition on training rides

### Running
- Walk breaks are fine, especially early in training
- Focus on consistency over speed
- Run/walk strategy is perfectly acceptable for your first race

### Transitions
- Practice T1 and T2 before race day
- Set up transition a few times at home
- Keep it simple—minimal gear

## Race Day Pacing Guide

| Discipline | Effort Level | Notes |
|------------|---------------|-------|
| Swim | Moderate | Start controlled, build |
| T1 | Steady | Don't rush, don't dally |
| Bike | Moderate | Push but save for run |
| T2 | Steady | Quick and organized |
| Run | Build | Start controlled, build effort |

*Your first triathlon is an achievement. Focus on finishing and enjoying the experience.*
