💡 Performance Training Tips

  • Respect the zones: Don't run tempo workouts at VO2 max pace. Each zone has a purpose.
  • Easy days easy: Recovery runs should feel genuinely easy. This is how you absorb hard workouts.
  • Quality over quantity: This plan maintains volume but increases intensity. Don't add extra miles.
  • Trust the taper: Reducing volume in the final weeks maintains sharpness while ensuring recovery.
  • Track your workouts: Log completed workouts to monitor if zones feel appropriate and adjust if needed.
  • Listen to your body: Persistent fatigue or pain means you need extra recovery, not more intensity.
