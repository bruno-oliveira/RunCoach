🥗 Nutrition Guidance for Runners

Pre-Run Fuel

Banana with peanut butter, oatmeal, or toast 30-60 minutes before running

Post-Run Recovery

Chocolate milk, protein smoothie, or Greek yogurt within 30 minutes of completion

Hydration

Drink 2-3L water daily. Add electrolytes for runs over 60 minutes

Meal Timing

Eat balanced meals every 3-4 hours. Include complex carbs and lean protein

🏥 Injury Prevention Tips
  • Always warm up with 5-10 minutes of light jogging before workouts
  • Cool down with stretching after every run, focusing on hamstrings and calves
  • Replace running shoes every 500-800km to prevent injury
  • Listen to your body - take extra rest days if you feel pain or excessive fatigue
  • Include foam rolling 2-3 times per week for muscle recovery
  • Gradually increase mileage by no more than 10% per week
  • Cross-train with swimming or cycling on rest days to maintain fitness
