One more thing to send workouts to your watch
  1. 1 Open Intervals.icu Settings → Connections and link your watch platform — Garmin, COROS, Wahoo, Suunto or Zwift. If you signed up to Intervals.icu with Garmin, this is already done.
  2. 2 In that connection, tick “Upload planned workouts”. This is the toggle that lets planned sessions travel from the calendar to your wrist.
Shortcut: if you signed up to Intervals.icu with your Garmin account, step 1 is already done. Just enable “Upload planned workouts” in that connection and you’re set.

Tip: set a Run threshold pace in Intervals.icu too — without it the watch shows “No target” for paced steps.

Polar and Apple Watch can’t receive planned workouts from any app — you’ll still get every session here in RunCoach.

Prefer not to link anything? Every workout has a .fit download button you can use to load sessions onto a Garmin over USB.
